Measure your foot

  1. Stand on a sheet of paper, on a flat floor, in the evening when feet are largest.
  2. Mark the back of your heel and the tip of your longest toe.
  3. Measure between the marks in centimetres, then repeat for the other foot.
  4. Measure the widest part of each foot.
  5. Send us both measurements, or a photo of the paper with a ruler beside it.

Craft & heritage

From the funeral ground to the palace court: footwear is part of how Ghanaians honour people and occasions.

A living tradition

Across Ghana, footwear is part of ceremonial dress. Sandals worn by chiefs and queen mothers carry authority and heritage, while funeral slippers help families mark a farewell with dignity. Today's makers carry these traditions forward with leather, cloth, beads and gold-tone details.

How a pair is made

These steps are typical for hand-made leather slippers. The designer should confirm or replace them with their own process.

  1. Design

    We agree the occasion, colours, emblem and fit.

  2. Choosing the leather

    Leather and trims are selected for the design.

  3. Cutting and shaping

    Soles, straps and panels are cut and shaped.

  4. Stitching

    Straps and panels are stitched by hand, with contrast thread where the design calls for it.

  5. Decorating

    Emblems, beads, cowrie shells, fringe or fabric inlays are added.

  6. Finishing and checking

    Edges are finished and each pair is checked for comfort and quality.
